IPromised and evidenced, by jurisdiction

Each jurisdiction has two separate bars on one shared scale in euros. The first bar sums public commitments that carry a stated amount. The second sums the amounts that sources state for what was allocated, awarded or delivered. The note under each pair says how many of the counted rows have been checked for delivery.

Evidenced amounts are often budgets, allocations or contract totals. They are not spending records. Many commitments have no amount that a source states for delivery, so the evidenced bar understates what has been delivered.
